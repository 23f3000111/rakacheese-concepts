Made by hand, every single day

Raka began in 2001 with two names, RA for Rajesh and KA for Kalpa Padia, and a love of good cheese. Highland milk from Nyeri, patient hands and recipes made for vegetarians. Twenty-five years on, Kalpa still leads the family business.
